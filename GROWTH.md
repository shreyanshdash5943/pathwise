# Pathwise growth plan (v2, researched)

A concrete plan to get users, grow the Instagram page, and pull organic traffic to the
website — built around what actually moves the needle, and around the growth loops
Pathwise already has.

Sources are listed at the end. Honest framing throughout: frameworks are well-established,
but many circulating **numbers come from vendors or secondary summaries** — treat those as
directional and verify your own analytics. Nobody can guarantee follower/traffic results;
this is the highest-probability playbook plus the metrics to know if it's working.

---

## 0. The one thing most people get wrong

You don't have a "get more followers" problem. You have a **"give people a reason to care,
then make it stupidly easy to act"** problem. Followers, traffic and signups are all
*downstream* of two things: a sharp offer, and consistent content that delivers real value
for free. Everything below serves those two.

The advantage: **Pathwise has growth loops built into the product.** Most startups buy every
user. You don't have to. Use that.

---

## 1. The offer (Hormozi: Value Equation + Grand Slam Offer)

From *$100M Offers*. First pick a **"starving crowd"** — a market with strong pain and a
reason to act. Students and early-career people who *don't know what to learn or do next*
are exactly that: high pain, high intent, huge volume. Good.

Then make the offer so good it's hard to compare. People judge value by:

```
        (Dream Outcome)  ×  (Perceived Likelihood of success)
Value = ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
             (Time Delay)  ×  (Effort & Sacrifice)
```

Push the top up, the bottom **down**:

| Lever | Weak | Pathwise's strong version |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | "Plan your career" | "Know exactly what to do today to land the job" |
| Likelihood | "Here's a course" | "A plan that adapts + proof employers can see + friends keeping you accountable" |
| Time delay | "Someday" | "First checklist in 2 minutes; your first win *today*" |
| Effort | "Do a 10-question quiz first" | "Skip the plan — just start a habit in 2 taps" ← you just shipped this; it's a big effort-reducer |

**Offer enhancers Hormozi recommends** (use sparingly, only when honest):
- **Risk reversal:** "Free forever. No card." (You already have this — it's your strongest card.)
- **Naming / specificity:** name the outcome, e.g. "The 90-day Job-Ready Plan."
- **Bonuses:** the public profile + PDF card + interview prep are effectively bonuses — *say so*.
- Scarcity/urgency: skip for now. Fake urgency on a free product reads as sleazy.

**Public one-liners to A/B test** (in bio, hooks, landing):
- "Stop guessing what to learn. Pathwise tells you what to do today — and proves it to employers."
- "A daily plan for the career you want. Free. Built for students."

## 2. The acquisition engine (Hormozi: the Core Four)

From *$100M Leads*. Every way to get attention is one of four channels, split by
warm/cold and one-to-one/one-to-many:

| | One-to-one | One-to-many |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** (people who know you) | **Warm outreach** — DM friends, classmates, your network | **Post free content** — to the people who follow you |
| **Cold** (strangers) | **Cold outreach** — DM strangers with fast value | **Paid ads** — pay for attention at scale |

The key discipline from the book: **master ONE channel before adding another; don't spread
thin.** Start warm, because warm converts fastest and teaches you what resonates.

**Pathwise sequencing (do in this order):**
1. **Warm outreach (now).** You and any cofounders personally message friends, classmates,
   juniors, college groups: "I built a free tool that tells you what to study each day —
   want to try it and tell me what's confusing?" This is unglamorous and it's how almost
   every product gets its first 100 users. It also feeds your product loops (pods).
2. **Free content (now, in parallel).** Instagram + short-form. This is the compounding
   engine — section 4. This is also "post free content" in the Core Four.
3. **Cold outreach (later).** Once content + warm are working, DM strangers in your niche
   with a specific, valuable hook. Low priority now.
4. **Paid ads (much later).** Only once you know your offer converts and you have money to
   lose learning. Don't start here.

**Content philosophy:** give away the *what* and *why* for free; the product is the
*done-for-you*. Your free value = the exact things users struggle with, which are also free
*samples* of Pathwise:
- "What to learn to become a frontend engineer in 2026 (full roadmap)"
- "I did LeetCode daily for 30 days — here's what changed" (you literally do this)
- "The 4 projects that get you hired as a data analyst"
- "How to answer 'tell me about yourself' using a project you built"
- "Why you quit every roadmap — and the fix"

Every one ends: "Pathwise turns this into a daily plan — free. Link in bio."

## 3. Pathwise's built-in growth loops (your real edge)

A referral loop's strength is the **K-factor = (invites per user) × (invite→signup rate)**.
K > 1 is true virality and is *rare* — most great products never hit it. But **K < 1 still
cuts your acquisition cost**, because every referred user is one you didn't have to chase.
So the goal isn't "go viral" — it's to raise K as high as you can and let content fill the top.

Your loops, ranked by leverage:
1. **Pods (invite codes)** — one user who starts a pod *brings 3–5 friends*. This is your
   highest-K mechanic, like Kahoot's classroom effect: the product is better *with* friends,
   so inviting is natural. *Action:* prompt "invite your study group" early and often.
2. **Public profiles (`/u/username`)** — every shared profile markets you, and each is an
   indexable page. Keep the "Made with Pathwise" footer on free profiles. *Action:* nudge
   users to share their profile right after their first proof of work (peak pride moment).
3. **PDF card** — recruiters/friends open it; it carries your link and footer.
4. **Shareable "win" moments** — "30-day streak", "first project shipped". *Small build:*
   one-tap "share my streak" image for Instagram Stories.
5. **OG images** — rich link previews when anyone shares your links in WhatsApp/DM/LinkedIn.
   *Small build.* Right now shared links look bare; this is cheap and high-impact.

> Three of these exist today. #4 and #5 are small builds I can do — they directly raise K
> and the share-through rate on every piece of content.

## 4. Instagram strategy

### 4a. The 2026 ranking reality (what actually gets reach)
Instagram runs **separate systems** for Reels, Feed, Stories, Explore. For *discovery*
(reaching non-followers), Reels is the engine, and the signals Mosseri has publicly
emphasised for 2026 are, in order:
1. **Watch time / completion** — early drop-off kills a Reel faster than anything. The first
   ~1–3 seconds are make-or-break; roughly half of viewers leave in 3 seconds, and IG now
   shows a **"view rate past 3 seconds"** metric in Reels Insights. Target a high 3-sec hold.
2. **Sends per reach (DM shares)** — people sharing your Reel into a DM is weighted *very*
   highly (some claim several × a like; unverified, but direction is clear). **Design for
   "who would someone send this to?"**
3. **Likes per reach.**
Plus: **saves** are weighted well above likes (great for roadmaps/lists), and **originality
matters** — reposted/watermarked clips from TikTok get suppressed. Post original footage.

How a Reel travels: IG shows it to a **small sample of non-followers**; if they watch
through / replay / send it, distribution **widens**; if they swipe away fast, it stalls and
rarely recovers. So optimise the hook and the "send-ability", not tricks.

### 4b. Page setup (the storefront — do first)
- **Name field** (searchable, ≠ handle): put keywords, e.g. "Pathwise · Career plans & daily habits".
- **Bio:** outcome + who it's for + CTA. e.g. "Know what to do today to land the job 🎯 /
  Free daily career plans + habit tracker for students / 👇 Start free" + clean website link.
- **Profile pic:** the logo mark, high-contrast, readable tiny.
- **Highlights (4–6, consistent covers):** Start here · Roadmaps · Proof of work · Pods · Wins · FAQ.
- **Pin 3 posts:** best explainer, best roadmap post, "Pathwise in 20 seconds" reel.

### 4c. Content pillars (rotate — don't post randomly)
1. **Roadmaps / "what to learn"** — most shareable + saveable; your top pillar.
2. **Daily accountability / habits** (LeetCode, DSA, consistency) — your authentic voice.
3. **Proof of work / portfolio / resume** — practical, high-intent.
4. **Interview prep** — searched constantly, high-intent.
5. **Build-in-public / behind the scenes** — trust + founder story.
6. **Student pain / motivation** — emotional, highly shareable ("it's okay to not know yet").

### 4d. Formats
- **Reels** = discovery. ~80% of effort.
- **Carousels** = saves/shares engine. Perfect for roadmaps and "5 projects that…" lists.
- **Stories** = retention for existing followers — polls, "building this today", share-backs, DMs.

### 4e. The Reel formula
1. **Hook in 1–2 seconds.** Script it word-for-word. Start at the payoff, not a slow intro.
   Patterns that work: tension, contrast (mistake→fix, before→after), one concrete promise.
   - "If you don't know what to learn for a tech job, watch this."
   - "You're not lazy. Your roadmap is broken."
2. **One idea per reel.** One takeaway. Don't teach everything.
3. **Retention:** captions on screen, a visual change every 2–3s, a clean first frame, works on mute.
4. **Engineer a send/save:** make it the kind of thing someone DMs to a friend ("you need this").
5. **CTA:** "Comment ROADMAP and I'll send the free plan" (comments + DMs are strong signals
   *and* start conversations). Pin a first comment with the link.

### 4f. Engagement tactics that compound
- **Reply to every comment in the first 60 min** (signals the post is worth pushing).
- **15 min/day genuinely commenting** on bigger niche accounts (career, coding, student life).
- **Ask a question** in every caption to manufacture comments.
- **"Comment X to get Y"** — highest-converting engagement + lead mechanic for this niche.

### 4g. Cadence (sustainable > heroic)
- Weeks 1–4: aim 1 reel/day if you can batch; otherwise 4 reels + 3 carousels/week, stories daily.
- Volume early = faster learning about what lands. **Batch on weekends:** script 7, film 7, edit 7.

## 5. Build-in-public (credible, but keep it honest)
X (Twitter) and Indie Hackers are the homes of #buildinpublic. It's a real discovery
channel, but the big multipliers you'll see quoted come from people selling tools/courses —
the well-supported takeaway is simply that **consistent, honest posting aids distribution,
and the product matters more than the narrative.** For a founder with no audience, start in
community-first spaces (Indie Hackers, which rewards detailed case studies with metrics),
then expand to X. Sharing what you ship *and* what breaks builds trust and a small loyal
following faster than polished hype.

## 6. Organic website traffic (SEO + the profile loop)
Your audience *searches* for what Pathwise answers. Slow to start, compounds, free.
1. **Programmatic role pages** (biggest SEO play): one page per role — "How to become a
   Frontend Engineer: roadmap, skills, projects" — from your existing `roles.ts` + template
   data. Dozens of pages targeting high-intent searches, each linking to signup. *A build I
   can do; reuses data you already have.*
2. **Public profiles are already indexable** — more users = more organic surface + backlinks.
3. **A simple `/blog`** mirroring your IG pillars. One source → IG + blog + LinkedIn.
4. **Basics:** sitemap, meta titles/descriptions, OG images, fast pages (already fast on Vercel).

## 7. The 90-day plan

**Weeks 1–2 — Foundation**
- Lock the offer one-liner (test 3 variants). Set up the IG page properly (name field, bio,
  highlights, 3 pins). Batch + publish first 10 reels + 4 carousels. Start **warm outreach**
  to your network. Build OG images + "share my streak" image. Draft a 30-slot content calendar.

**Weeks 3–6 — Volume + loops**
- Post daily; stories daily. Turn on in-product nudges ("share your profile", "invite your pod").
- 15 min/day niche commenting. Ship 5–10 programmatic role pages; submit sitemap.
- Track which hooks/pillars earn **saves + sends**; double down on the top 20%.

**Weeks 7–12 — Compound + convert**
- Keep volume; cut the bottom 20% of formats, scale the winners.
- Launch a college **ambassador** angle (your tier-2/3 wedge): shout out active pod creators,
  give them early Pro.
- Repurpose top carousels into blog guides. Add real social proof *once true* (user wins,
  pod activity, a genuine testimonial).

## 8. What "concrete results" realistically look like
- **Weeks 1–4:** mostly flat. You're learning what lands. Judge by *reps shipped*, not followers.
- **Weeks 4–8:** a few posts outperform (saves/sends climb). Followers tick up. First IG signups.
- **Weeks 8–12+:** with consistency, 1–2 reels break out; compounding starts. SEO is a 3–6
  month curve.
- **The real flywheel is the product loop:** users → public profiles + pods → their friends →
  more users. Content fills the top; the loops multiply it.

**Metrics (weekly):**
- Leading: posts shipped, **saves + sends** per post, 3-sec hold rate, profile visits, comments/DMs, link clicks.
- Lagging: followers, website visits, signups, pods created, profiles shared.
- Sends and saves > likes. DMs and comments > raw reach. Signups > everything.

---

## 9. Honest cautions
- **No overnight virality.** K > 1 is rare; don't bank on it. Consistency + loops win.
- **Don't buy followers/engagement** — it poisons your reach signal.
- **Don't fake social proof** (fake testimonials, fake "trusted by", fake urgency). Trust landmine.
- **Vanity metrics lie.** 500 users who sign up > 10k followers who don't.
